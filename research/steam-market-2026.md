# Doorbuster: Steam market research (October 2026)

Done after the game was built, which was the wrong order. Numbers are from
third-party trackers and consultants (review counts as a sales proxy), not
Valve. Treat them as ranges.

## 1. The genre is growing fast and getting crowded

| Year | Bullet Heaven releases on Steam |
|---|---|
| 2023 | 37 |
| 2024 | 69 |
| 2025 | 103 |
| 2026 to Sep 27 | 329 (same weeks of 2025: 65) |

Some of the 2026 jump is Steam's May 2026 tag overhaul, but supply is clearly
multiplying, and 753 more pages are unreleased. ([steampageanalyzer][spa])

It still beats the Steam average. Games released Jan 2024 to Jun 2025:

| | Bullet Heaven | All Steam |
|---|---|---|
| reached 100 reviews | 41.1% | 17.3% |
| reached 1,000 reviews (about $150k gross) | 13.1% (8 to 21%) | 4.2% |
| median reviews | 75 | 11 |

([steampageanalyzer][spa]) A stricter count of true Vampire Survivors-likes
found only one (Deep Rock Galactic Survivors) passing 1,000 reviews in 2024,
down from 17 in 2023. ([How To Market A Game via GameDev Reports][htmag24])
The two disagree on the definition; both say a plain clone is not enough.

## 2. Every breakout changes a core mechanic, not just the theme

| Game | Twist on the formula | Result | Price |
|---|---|---|---|
| BALL x PIT (Oct 2025) | brick-breaker × survivors × base building | 2M+ sold ([Push Square][ballxpit]) | $14.99 |
| Megabonk (2025) | 3D, jumping and sliding; chaotic, streamable, funny | 1M in two weeks, 117k concurrent ([Notebookcheck][megabonk]) | $9.99 |
| The Spell Brigade | 1–4 player online co-op with friendly fire | 5,000+ reviews in 2026 ([steampageanalyzer][spa]) | $12.99 |

A studio head summing up 2025: the genre grows through "new synergies of core
mechanics, new metas, new settings". ([WN Hub][wnhub]) Setting is one of the
three, never the only one. Zukowski's framing: associate with an established
genre, then visibly separate from the pile. ([Kotaku][kotaku])

**Doorbuster today:** classic Vampire Survivors mechanics with a retail skin.
Good theme, good humor, no mechanical twist. That's the gap.

## 3. Price: $4.99 was too low

- 2024–25 cohort: the $5–9.99 band did best (52% reached 100 reviews, 14% reached
  1,000), versus under $5 (24% and 9%). ([steampageanalyzer][spa])
- The 2025–26 hits sit at $9.99–14.99 (table above).
- Correlation, not cause: pricier games have more content. But nothing says the
  genre needs to be $3.99.

**Revised:** $7.99 at launch with the current content; $9.99 if the hook below ships.

## 4. Wishlist math sets the ceiling

- **Popular Upcoming** needs about 7,000–10,000 wishlists in the last week; it's
  driven by velocity, and the slot itself brings about 1,000 wishlists a day.
  ([How To Market A Game][htmag-wl])
- **First-week conversion:** median about 0.15× wishlists for games with 25k+
  wishlists (Sep 2024–Sep 2025), about 0.10× above $10; spread is 10× either
  way. ([GameDiscoverCo via GameDev Reports][gdco])
- **Next Fest** multiplies, it doesn't create: median 806 wishlists earned in
  Feb 2026 (1,079 a year earlier); games that enter with more earn more.
  Don't debut the demo during the fest. ([presskit.gg][nextfest], [steampageanalyzer][snf])

| Wishlists at launch | Week-one sales at 0.10–0.15× | Week-one gross at $7.99 |
|---|---|---|
| 2,000 | 200–300 | $1.6k–2.4k |
| 10,000 | 1,000–1,500 | $8k–12k |
| 50,000 | 5,000–7,500 | $40k–60k |

Steam keeps 30%, then refunds, VAT and regional pricing. Lifetime is commonly
a few times week one. Breakouts (Megabonk) come from streamers, not wishlists.

## 5. What this means for Doorbuster

1. **Find a mechanical hook that only works in a store**, then test it before
   building more content. Three candidates, cheapest to test first:
   - **Restock or Riot:** shelves empty as shoppers grab stock; empty aisles
     make the crowd angrier and faster. You keep killing, but you also have to
     run restocks. A positional objective that fights the kite loop.
     Survivors × light store management, like BALL x PIT's base building.
   - **Couch / online co-op "staff"** (2–4 workers, friendly fire from carts and
     spills). Proven by The Spell Brigade; Doorbuster already has gamepads.
   - **Physics crowd:** shoppers as a pushable crowd (carts plowing lines,
     pile-ups, domino falls). Megabonk-style chaos for streamers.
2. **Test before building:** a 1-day prototype of the top candidate, a 15-second
   GIF and a capsule mock, shown to people (subreddits, Discord, X) and compared
   on click/comment rate against plain Doorbuster footage.
3. **Coming Soon page now.** Wishlists take months; the page costs nothing to keep up.
4. **Price $7.99–9.99**, not $3.99.
5. **Target 10k wishlists before launch**; if under 7k by the planned date,
   move the date.
6. **Streamers over ads:** send keys to small and mid survivors-like streamers;
   the genre's breakouts spread through chaotic, funny clips.
