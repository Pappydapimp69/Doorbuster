# Doorbuster — Steam store page copy

## Short description (max 300 chars)

It's 5:00 AM on Black Friday and you're the only one working the floor.
Doorbuster is a survivors-like where your weapons fire on their own: price
guns, runaway carts, PA announcements. Move, level up, and survive the crowd
until 11:00 AM.

## About this game

**The doors open at 5:00 AM. You clock out at 11:00. Everything in between is your problem.**

Doorbuster is a bite-sized survivors-like set in the worst shift of the year.
Hundreds of shoppers pour through the doors and you've got a name tag, a
price gun, and a dream. Weapons fire automatically — you move, pick
upgrades, and build something that can hold the line.

**Arm yourself from the shelves**
- 11 weapons: Price Gun, Box Cutter, Runaway Carts, Wet Floor, PA System,
  Coupon Storm, Barcode Laser, Gift Card, Fire Extinguisher, Anti-Theft
  Alarm and a Rotisserie Chicken that explodes. Somehow.
- 6 passives and 6 evolutions — max a weapon, own the right item, and get
  promoted to the Clearance Cannon, Cart Train, or Store-Wide Announcement.

**Know your customers**
- Shoppers, Speed Walkers, Cart Pushers, Coupon Clippers, Scalpers, and Line
  Campers who split into three people when you finally knock their tent over.
- Customers who would like to speak to your manager. Your manager is busy.
- The Regional Manager. He'd like to circle back.

**Ten-minute shifts, endless overtime**
- A full run is a 6-hour shift squeezed into 10 minutes.
- Survive it and keep going: Overtime never ends, it just gets worse.
- Doorbuster Rushes and Flash Sales hit at set times. Find the gap.

**Earn tips, spend them in the Break Room**
- Permanent perks, four staff to hire (Stocker, Cashier, Security, Night
  Janitor), and a second department to unlock: Electronics, after midnight.

**Built for the couch and the Deck**
- Full gamepad support, readable at handheld size, toggles for screen shake
  and damage numbers.

language in shop announcements ("crap" level at most). Fill the IARC
questionnaire in Steamworks accordingly.
